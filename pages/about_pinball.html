    Pinball

    How to play:

    Click the left or right side of the screen to activate the corresponding flippers if you're playing on mobile.

    Click left arrow or "A" key to activate the left flippers and right arrow or "D" key for the right flippers.

    Press space to launch the ball.


    Some assets used in this game were made by Rounddroid.
