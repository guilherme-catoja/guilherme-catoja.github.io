About me

Since I was a kid I had a passion for video games, over time that passion made me want to create my own video games.

My biggest strength as a Game Designer is conceptualizing and developing game mechanics and systems that deliver the best possible experience to the players.

I'm skilled in various programming languages, mainly python and java, and I have 5 years of experience developing my own videogames using various game engines, such as Godot, Unity and Construct; however I've also developed some games without any engine.

I have a wide range of experience on various videogame design methodologies such as system design and level design; but also methodologies related to software development, such as design thinking and design sprinting.

My hobbies are mainly playing video games, but I also like to play guitar and compose music. I also draw pixel art when I need assets I can't find online.
