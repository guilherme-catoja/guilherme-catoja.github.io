    Adventure in Gridlandia

    Grade: 17,5/20

    Adventure in Gridlandia is an RPG made in Microsoft Excel as the final project for Excel class.

    Download on itch.io

    Synopsis

    This game takes place in Gridlandia, a peaceful nation that got engulfed in darkness after the Evil King of Macros became the ruler.

    Now it's up to the player to gather all 7 Macros and defeat the Evil King.

    Game Design

    The idea for this game started when our group was discussing ideas for our final Excel project.

    The code is entirely written in VBA macros, from the turn based combat to encounter triggers and moving through the world.

    The game has a total of 3 databases: 1 for the enemies stats, other for the allies stats and the last one for the quests, which includes dialogue lines, objectives and player location triggers.

    All characters are drawn in Ascii art to fit the theme of Excel and, since this is a fantasy game, the characters also fit the fantasy genre.
